# Instructional Visual — Source & License Register

First-batch instructional visuals for the Step-by-Step Guide (Implementation Task 5).
These are distinct from the category images (see `public/images/CREDITS.md`): category
images identify the emergency; these demonstrate the physical action of a specific
approved step.

Every visual was reviewed against its step's approved text (procedure, position, hand
placement, direction, safety, anatomy, consistency) before being attached. All were
downloaded and stored locally — no hotlinking. Accessed 12 September 2026.
